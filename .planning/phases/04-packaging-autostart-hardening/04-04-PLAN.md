  <behavior>
    - Policy: Seen resets the miss counter and yields None.
    - Policy: Inconclusive (SendInput blocked, locked session, probe skipped) never increments misses and yields None.
    - Policy: NotSeen increments misses; reaching MissThreshold (2) yields Reinstall and resets misses.
    - Policy: at most MaxReinstalls (5) reinstalls per RollingWindow (10 min, injectable clock); beyond that yields Escalate (log error, stop reinstalling) so a permanently failing environment does not spin.
    - Watchdog.RunOnceAsync probes once, applies the policy, invokes the reinstall callback only on Reinstall, and returns the action; a probe or callback exception is caught, logged via an injected Action of string, counted as Inconclusive, and never escapes the loop.
    - Watchdog.RunAsync loops with PeriodicTimer (default 30 s) until cancelled, and skips probing while IsRecordingActive is true or while a reinstall was requested less than one interval ago (allow the new hook to settle).
  </behavior>
