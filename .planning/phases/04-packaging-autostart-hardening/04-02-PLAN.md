# Plan 04-02: Power & Session Resilience (Sleep/Resume, Lock/Unlock) & Hook Watchdog

**Phase**: 04 - Packaging, Autostart & Hardening  
**Wave**: 2  
**Requirements**: DIST-03  

## Objective
Ensure the global low-level keyboard hook and dictation loop survive Windows sleep/resume cycles, workstation lock/unlock events, and prolonged uptime by listening to `WM_POWERBROADCAST` and `WM_WTSSESSION_CHANGE`, and automatically reinstalling the hook if Windows drops it during sleep or lock.

## Requirements Covered
- **DIST-03**: App keeps working after sleep/resume, lock/unlock and long uptime (hook watchdog/reinstall).

## Architecture & Design
1. **Power & Session Listener (`Somning.Platform.Windows.Power.PowerSessionListener`)**:
   - Hidden message window registering for:
     - `WM_POWERBROADCAST`:
       - `PBT_APMSUSPEND`: pauses background health probes, discards interrupted active captures safely.
       - `PBT_APMRESUMEAUTOMATIC` / `PBT_APMRESUMESUSPEND`: signals system resumed.
     - `WM_WTSSESSION_CHANGE` via `WTSRegisterSessionNotification(hWnd, NOTIFY_FOR_THIS_SESSION)`:
       - `WTS_SESSION_LOCK`: marks session locked.
       - `WTS_SESSION_UNLOCK`: triggers hook verification and automatic re-hook.
2. **Hook Watchdog & Reinstaller (`Somning.Platform.Windows.Hook.KeyboardHookWatchdog`)**:
   - Windows is known to silently unhook low-level hooks (`WH_KEYBOARD_LL`) if a hook callback takes too long or during power transitions.
   - Watchdog provides:
     - `ReinstallHook()`: unhooks existing handle and calls `SetWindowsHookEx` again on the dedicated hook thread.
     - Automatically invoked on `Resume` and `SessionUnlock`.
3. **Integration with `KeyboardHook` & `AppComposition`**:
   - `KeyboardHook` exposes `Reinstall()` or receives wake/unlock notifications from `PowerSessionListener`.
4. **Integration Tests**:
   - `PowerSessionIntegrationTests`: sends simulated `WM_POWERBROADCAST` and `WM_WTSSESSION_CHANGE` messages to the message window, asserts hook handle is refreshed and keyboard event channel remains operational.

## Tasks
- **Task 1**: Implement `PowerSessionListener` and hook reinstallation watchdog.
- **Task 2**: Integration tests simulating sleep, resume, lock, unlock and hook health verification.
